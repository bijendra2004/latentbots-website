Our Story

LatentBots started with a simple question: what if useful notifications and automation could feel calm instead of demanding?

We built a family of intelligent WhatsApp bots to deliver the things that matter—from priority emails and system alerts to daily morning digests—in the place people already check every day: WhatsApp.

No inbox overload. No endless dashboard. Just clear, timely messages that help you stay informed and get back to your life.
